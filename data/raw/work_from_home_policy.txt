Human Resources Department

WORK-FROM-HOME POLICY Version: 1.0 Effective Date: 01 January 2025
Classification: Internal Use Only

1.  PURPOSE This Work-from-Home (WFH) Policy defines the guidelines for
    employees who are approved to work remotely. The objective is to
    maintain productivity, collaboration, information security, and
    business continuity while providing workplace flexibility.

2.  SCOPE This policy applies to permanent, probationary, and contract
    employees whose roles are approved for remote work by their
    Reporting Manager and Human Resources.

3.  ELIGIBILITY

-   Remote work is not an automatic employee benefit.
-   Approval depends on business requirements.
-   Employees must have a stable internet connection and an approved
    work environment.

4.  WORKING HOURS Employees must follow the standard office schedule
    unless otherwise approved.

-   Office Hours: 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM
-   Lunch Break: 1 Hour
-   Employees should be available during core business hours.

5.  ATTENDANCE Employees must:

-   Mark attendance through the HR Attendance System.
-   Join daily stand-up meetings.
-   Inform the Reporting Manager before taking leave.
-   Respond to official communication within a reasonable time.

6.  PRODUCTIVITY EXPECTATIONS Employees are expected to:

-   Complete assigned work within deadlines.
-   Attend scheduled meetings.
-   Maintain regular communication with the team.
-   Update task progress in the project management system.

7.  INFORMATION SECURITY

-   Use only company-approved devices whenever possible.
-   Do not share confidential company information.
-   Lock the computer when away from the workstation.
-   Store official documents only in approved repositories.
-   Report security incidents immediately.

8.  COMMUNICATION Communication channels include company email,
    Microsoft Teams/Slack, video meetings, and the approved project
    management platform. Employees should keep their status updated
    during working hours.

9.  EQUIPMENT Employees are responsible for protecting company-issued
    laptops, chargers, and accessories. Damage or loss must be reported
    immediately.

10. POLICY VIOLATIONS Examples include:

-   Falsifying attendance.
-   Sharing confidential information.
-   Repeated unavailability during working hours.
-   Failure to complete assigned work without valid reasons.

11. REVISION HISTORY Version 1.0 - Initial Work-from-Home Policy
    Release.

End of Document
