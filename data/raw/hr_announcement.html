Subject: Updated Hybrid Work Schedule and Employee Wellness Initiative

Dear Employees,

The Human Resources Department is pleased to announce an updated hybrid work schedule and a new employee wellness initiative. These changes are being introduced to improve collaboration, employee well-being, and work-life balance while ensuring that project delivery and customer commitments remain unaffected.

1. Updated Hybrid Work Schedule

Effective from 01 July 2025, eligible employees may work remotely for up to two days per week, subject to manager approval and project requirements.

Employee Category Remote Work Eligibility Approval Required
Permanent Employees Up to 2 days per week Reporting Manager
Probationary Employees Based on manager discretion Manager and HR
Contract Employees As per contract terms Department Head
Interns Not eligible unless specifically approved HR and Reporting Manager

2. Attendance Requirements

  • Employees must mark attendance through the approved HR system.
  • Core availability hours are from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
  • Employees must attend scheduled meetings and respond to official communication.
  • Any deviation from the approved work schedule must be communicated in advance.

3. Employee Wellness Initiative

The company is introducing a monthly wellness program beginning in July 2025. The program will include awareness sessions, stress management workshops, ergonomic guidance, and voluntary fitness activities.

  • Monthly wellness session
  • Quarterly health awareness workshop
  • Optional guided meditation session
  • Ergonomic workstation guidance
  • Confidential employee support channel

4. Manager Responsibilities

  • Review hybrid-work requests fairly and promptly.
  • Ensure team coverage and project continuity.
  • Monitor deliverables rather than physical presence alone.
  • Escalate repeated attendance or productivity concerns to HR.
Important: This announcement does not replace the existing Work-from-Home Policy or Attendance Policy. Employees must continue to follow all applicable HR and information-security requirements.

5. Effective Date and Support

The revised schedule becomes effective on 01 July 2025. Employees with questions should contact the Human Resources Department or their Reporting Manager.
